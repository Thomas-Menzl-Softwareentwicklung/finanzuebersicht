Keep track of your income, expenses, and budgets with Meine Finanzübersicht. Log transactions, organize recurring payments, and follow your savings goals — without a bank login, ads, or tracking.

YOUR EVERYDAY EXPENSE TRACKER
See where your money goes with monthly and yearly overviews, categories, and budgets. Manage multiple accounts and record transfers between them. Add income and expenses directly in the app, search your transactions, and use templates for frequent entries.

RECURRING PAYMENTS AND SAVINGS GOALS
Keep track of your salary, rent, and other recurring transactions. Reminders help you record payments when they are due. Follow your progress toward savings goals. The app does not execute bank transfers.

LOCAL STORAGE, NO SIGN-UP
Your financial data is stored on your device. No additional app account is required. Use the backup feature to save your data and restore it when needed.

START FOR FREE
Record unlimited transactions and use up to 3 accounts, 8 recurring transactions, 1 savings goal, and 3 transaction templates.

UNLOCK PRO WITH A ONE-TIME PURCHASE
Pro gives you unlimited accounts, recurring transactions, savings goals, and templates. Import CSV files with column mapping, a preview, automatic category suggestions, and duplicate detection. Plan ahead with the 30-day cash-flow forecast and capture expenses using the iPhone Home Screen widget.

OPTIONAL ICLOUD SYNC
Keep your data up to date across iPhone, iPad, and Mac using your private iCloud. Sync is a separate yearly subscription and does not require Pro. Local use does not require a subscription.

For iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Available in German and English. With Dark Mode and VoiceOver support.
